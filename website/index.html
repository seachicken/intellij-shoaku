WHY SHOAKU

AI can write the code.
You still have to
live with it.

Useful software lasts longer than the prompt that created it. To maintain it with confidence, someone needs to understand the choices inside.

Shoaku keeps that understanding with the team.

FOR EXPERIENCED DEVELOPERS

Keep the craft in coding.

Stay close to the problem, test your instincts, and enjoy making the decisions that matter—without turning the whole task over to an agent.

FOR DEVELOPERS BUILDING EXPERIENCE

Build judgment, not dependency.

Work through the why behind the code. Questions and comparison help turn each task into durable knowledge you can carry forward.

THE SHOAKU APPROACH

Understand as you build.
Compare before you finish.

Shoaku supports your understanding while an agent explores,
then brings both perspectives together at the end.

01 / WORK AT YOUR PACE

Build understanding,
step by step.

Shoaku breaks the task into smaller steps, so you can work through each one at your own pace. Each loop of asking, trying, and reflecting builds the understanding that carries you toward completion.

02 / SEPARATE WORK, SHARED REVIEW

Work separately.
Compare at the end.

You and the agent code in separate workspaces, so its exploration never interrupts your pace. At the end, compare both implementations to find gaps, challenge assumptions, and strengthen the result.
